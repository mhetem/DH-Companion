## What's new

### New classes, ancestries and communities, the Dread domain, and transformations

Hilt now carries the Daggerheart SRD 2.0 player content.

- **Assassin, Brawler, Warlock and Witch**, each with both subclasses, in the character wizard
  and as multiclass options.
- **Six new ancestries**: Aetheris, Gnome, and the four Elemental Kin: Earthkin, Emberkin,
  Skykin and Tidekin. Earthkin's Stoneskin counts toward your Armor Score and thresholds, and
  Skykin's Eye of the Storm is a toggle on the Defenses card.
- **Six new communities**: Duneborne, Freeborne, Frostborne, Hearthborne, Reborne and
  Warborne.
- **The Dread domain**: all 21 cards, for Warlocks and Witches.
- **Transformations**: Demigod, Ghost, Reanimated, Shapeshifter, Vampire and Werewolf. Your GM
  grants these, so you take one from the sheet, next to your ancestry and community.
- **Martial Stances** for Martial Artists: mark the stances you know, spend Focus to shift into
  one, and the sheet drops you out on Severe damage.
- **Class trackers**: a Warlock's Favor (take Favor instead of Hope after a roll, or show
  tribute on a rest), a Martial Artist's Focus with a Refocus roll, the Brawler's Combo Die as a
  level-up option and a combo-strike roll, and Brawler's Strike in the dice panel while you're
  barehanded.

### The sheet does the arithmetic

- **Pick gear from the SRD tables.** In Inventory, typing a name suggests every SRD weapon,
  armor, item and consumable; anything else is still kept as your own item.
- **Equipping armor sets your Armor Score and thresholds**, and gear such as a shield's
  "+1 to Armor Score" or heavy armor's "-1 to Evasion" counts while it's equipped. Armor that
  scales with you counts too: Mage Robes add your Spellcast trait to your thresholds,
  Granminster's Finery adds your Presence to your Armor Score, and the Rune-Forged Exosuit adds
  your tier. A two-handed weapon takes both hands.
- **Abilities count themselves in.** Subclass features like Unwavering or Rugged, the Galapa's
  Shell, Bare Bones, and loadout cards like Fortified Armor or Untouchable adjust your
  defenses as you level, equip and swap cards. The Defenses card lists everything it counted.
- **What's true right now** shows separately: Eldritch Flesh, Conjure Shield, your stance and
  your Beastform, plus toggles for timed effects like Patron's Mantle, Rogue's Dodge,
  Channeling or Frenzy. They change the Evasion and thresholds a hit is checked against, then
  switch off on the hit or rest that ends them.
- **Taking damage:** type the damage you took and the sheet marks the right number of Hit
  Points, optionally through an Armor Slot, and tells you when it's your last.
- **Weapon rolls:** every equipped SRD weapon gets Attack and Damage buttons in the dice panel.
- **Multiclassing and a domain card in the same level-up** now offers the new class's cards.

### For the GM

- **Turn, zoom and drag what's on the player window**, from the GM's preview or on the player
  window itself with the mouse wheel, a drag, or the R, +, - and 0 keys. Each map remembers
  how you framed it.
- **Countdowns that aren't in any campaign** can be moved into one from its Countdowns tab.

### Good to know

- **Existing characters keep the numbers you typed** until their gear or abilities change. To
  have armor you already carry set your defenses, edit it in Inventory and pick it from the SRD
  list.
- **Hilt now credits the Daggerheart SRD 2.0**, which builds on 1.0.
- **Updating from 2.1.0:** the Windows installer, the macOS app and the AppImage update
  themselves from **Settings & data → Check for updates → Update**. Your data carries over, and
  the database upgrades itself the first time the new version opens.
